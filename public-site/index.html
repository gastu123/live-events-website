Frequently asked questions

What happens after payment?

After payment verification, fulfilment information will be provided separately. No ticket is generated or delivered during this ordering phase.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Cancellation and exchange eligibility depends on the event terms shown during checkout.

How long does payment verification take?

Every payment is reviewed manually after you submit the requested payment proof. Review time depends on when the receiving account can be checked.
